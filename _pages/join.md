# Join Us

**The RIA Lab is new and hiring at every level.** We are recruiting PhD students, Master's students, and undergraduate researchers who want to work on interactive robot learning, uncertainty quantification, and human-AI collaboration.

You do not need to have done robotics before. We look for people with strong fundamentals in machine learning, probability, or optimization; comfort writing real code; and genuine curiosity about how people and autonomous systems work together. 

### PhD students

Apply to the PhD program in Computer Science or Informatics at the [Luddy School of Informatics, Computing, and Engineering](https://luddy.indiana.edu/) and **name me in your application**. Applications are reviewed by the department, so applying to the program is the necessary step, but please also send a short email.


### Master's students and undergraduates at IU

If you are already at Indiana University and want to get involved in research, email me with your CV.
