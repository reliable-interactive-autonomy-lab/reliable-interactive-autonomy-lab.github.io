Research

Our research focuses on algorithms for robots that learn from human feedback efficiently, quantify their own uncertainty honestly, and coordinate with human partners fluently.

Current Projects

Explainable Robot Failure Detection

Grounding robot failure prediction in causal concept bottleneck models, so a robot can say which concepts drove a predicted failure and what would change the outcome. Presented at the Human-Robot Dialogue workshop at IROS 2026.

Contact: Mihir Parekh (mihparek [at] iu.edu)
Communicating Visuomotor Policy Attention

Analyzing what a policy's internal visual representations encode and turning them into stage-relevant guidance for human teachers during interactive corrections. Presented at the Rethinking Uncertainty workshop at IROS 2026.

Contact: Jiahe Liu (jl456 [at] iu.edu)
End-User Customization of Robot Behavior

Understanding users' needs, preferences, and interactions with robots, and designing interfaces that let non-experts personalize and shape robot behavior.

Contact: Sunny Qiao (qiao [at] iu.edu)
Immersive Interfaces for Human-Robot Interaction

Bringing user-centered design methods and immersive technologies to interfaces for observing, teaching, and collaborating with robots.

Contact: JeongTak Cho (jtcho [at] iu.edu)
Human-in-the-Loop Interaction Design

User research and strategic design for human-in-the-loop robot learning: studying how people actually intervene, correct, and collaborate with learning robots to inform interaction design.

Contact: Pradyumna Satheesh (psathees [at] iu.edu)
